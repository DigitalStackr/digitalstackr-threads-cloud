DigitalStackr Poster

Internal content scheduling tool for the DigitalStackr brand.

DigitalStackr Poster is a private, self-hosted scheduling tool. It lets the DigitalStackr team queue short-form videos and images in advance and publish them automatically to the brand's own social accounts at planned times.

What it does

  • Stores a schedule of the brand's own content (caption + media file).
  • At each scheduled time, publishes that content to the brand's connected accounts.
  • Retries automatically if a platform is temporarily unavailable.

Who it is for

It is used solely by the DigitalStackr brand owner to post the brand's own content to the brand's own accounts. It is not a consumer product, it has no public sign-up, and it does not post on behalf of third parties.
